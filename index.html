Curriculum

Three Levels.
One Mission.

Live coaching sessions designed to take you from your first stream to a full-time creator career — at whatever level you're starting from.

🟣 Beginner
Stream Starter
Zero experience needed. We walk you through everything from setup to going live for the first time.
  • Setting up your streaming software (OBS/Streamlabs)
  • Camera, mic & lighting setup on any budget
  • Choosing your niche and platform (Twitch vs YouTube)
  • Your first stream — going live with confidence
  • Building your channel page & branding basics
  • Understanding analytics and viewer engagement
⭐ Intermediate
Audience Builder
You're streaming — now let's grow. Learn how to attract viewers, build community, and start monetizing.
  • Content strategy & consistency systems
  • Social media growth for streamers (TikTok, Instagram, X)
  • Community building & Discord management
  • Monetization basics — subs, bits, donations, sponsors
  • Collaboration & networking with other creators
  • Stream overlays, alerts & professional production
🔥 Advanced
Creator Pro
Turn your stream into a business. Brand deals, scaling income, and building a lasting creator career.
  • Full-time creator business model & income diversification
  • Brand deal negotiation & sponsorship outreach
  • Merchandise & digital product creation
  • YouTube strategy for long-form content alongside streaming
  • Advanced audience retention & algorithm mastery
  • Personal brand building & long-term career planning
